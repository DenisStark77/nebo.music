Whose Script Is It?

A 5-minute quiz: which family script is loudest in your relationships right now.

Answer as things are, not as they “should” be. The quiz compares four common family scripts – three statements each, 12 in total. The script names appear after scoring, so they don’t nudge your answers.

0 of 12

This is a reflective quiz I wrote. It draws on ideas from systemic family constellations – Bert Hellinger’s “orders of love,” hidden loyalty to the family, and parentification (when a child becomes a parent’s emotional support). It is not a validated psychological test and not a diagnosis. If things feel very heavy right now, please start with a therapist or doctor. Your answers aren’t sent anywhere – scoring happens right in your browser.

Your result

0–2 – quiet, barely affects you. 3–4 – noticeable: takes the wheel in some situations. 5–6 – loud: you’ll probably recognize yourself.

Two scripts scored the same – that’s common. Scripts intertwine: “I’ll do it myself” often lives right next to “The Rescuer.”
