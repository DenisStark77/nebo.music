Sound familiar?

  • “Same guy, different name.”
  • “I love my mom, but after one phone call I need a week to recover.”
  • “I carry everyone – and there’s no one to lean on.”
  • “We keep having the exact same fight.”
  • “I see myself in my daughter – and it scares me.”
  • “I get it in my head. Nothing changes.”

If even one of these is you – it’s not bad luck and it’s not “just who you are.” Most often it’s a family script: a role you once took on out of love. You can see it – and step out of it.

How a constellation works. No woo-woo

  1. You name what keeps happening. Say: “why do I always end up alone?”
  2. You choose stand-ins – group members who represent you and your family. On Zoom, each is in their own window; you’re at home.
  3. We watch who turns toward whom, who turns away, who feels heavy. The stand-ins know nothing about you – and still often feel exactly what you recognize.
  4. One sentence is said, and something shifts. You don’t need to believe in anything – you just see your family from the outside.

Who leads it

I’m Jane Stark. I hold a PhD in Economics, and since 2021 I’ve been an active member of Constellators International.

From 2010 to 2012 I trained in constellations with Sergey Kazansky in St. Petersburg, then with Boris Kholmyansky, and completed an online course with Tatiana Framuza. I’ve facilitated more than 300 constellations since 2021, in groups and 1:1, online and offline at the retreats.

I studied in person with Grand masters: working with the body with B. K. S. Iyengar, breathing practices with Mantak Chia, working with attention with Krishna Das. I use this knowledge and NVC practices in my sessions.

My life divides into “before” and “after” I discovered constellations in 2010. I understood: there’s no such thing as laziness – there’s only work you don’t love, chosen by other people’s ideas of how things should be – someone else’s script. Same insights and changes followed in the realm of relationships, health and overall wellbeing.

Once I saw that, my bans on creativity fell away. I started singing, writing music, painting in oils and publishing books. I’ve moved to a new country three times and built a life and a reputation from scratch. I speak six languages, and in Dubai I directed the Noble Poetry Club Theatre. Today I live in an off grid community in Costa Rica.

Questions

Is this woo-woo? A cult?

No. We don’t believe in anything and we don’t “clear” anything. You simply look at your family from the outside, and what you do with it is your choice.

I’d feel embarrassed opening up to strangers

There are six of us, the same confidentiality agreement applies to everyone, and nobody is from your circle. You don’t need to tell your whole family story – your question is enough.

Does this really work online?

On Zoom the stand-ins are the same real people – each in their own window. And you’re at home, in your own safe place.

What if my turn comes last?

The work starts in the first session: in other people’s constellations, as a stand-in or witness, your own charge eases too. People often arrive at their own constellation with a different question than they started with.

What if it makes things worse?

I work gently and don’t rush. Between sessions you have a week to live with what you saw, and the last session is for integration. And honestly: this group isn’t for everyone – see below.

$450 feels like a lot

It’s six weeks for roughly the price of one private constellation: your own constellation plus five others. If the amount isn’t possible for you right now, write to me about the open spot by donation.

Who this isn’t for

  • If you’re in an acute state right now: severe depression, a flare-up of a psychiatric condition, the first months after a heavy loss. Please start with a doctor or therapist – the group can wait.
  • If you want the constellation to “fix” someone else – your partner, your mom, your child.
  • If you expect an instant result in one evening.
  • If you can’t come to the sessions regularly: the group holds because all six people show up.
